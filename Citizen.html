The Problem

📚

Weak Foundational Learning

Anganwadi centres lack structured, psychology-based pedagogy for Listening, Speaking, Reading & Writing (ages 4–6).

🥦

Malnutrition & Health Gaps

Junk food, little use of local seasonal nutrition and no regular check-ups hinder growth.

🎓

Dropouts, Child Marriage & Labour

No accountability or vocational pathway till 18 pushes weak students out of school.

📱

Screen Addiction

Unsupervised smartphone use harms focus and mental wellbeing of minors.

🗂️

Fragmented Records

Aadhaar, Voter ID, mark sheets and bank details are scattered, causing delays and leakages.

💡

Our Answer

A two-tier system: 4S foundation in childhood + Citizen Card accountability in adulthood.
